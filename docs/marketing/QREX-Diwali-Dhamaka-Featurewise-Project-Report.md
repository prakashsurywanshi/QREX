- **System:** Activity logged, Task created & Notification sent
- **Result:** Right person gets notified immediately

- **Result:** Smooth scheduling & tracking of site visits

<details>

- **Result:** All negotiations tracked on a single visual board

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>STEP 5: BOOKING & PAYMENT TRACKING</b></summary>

- **System:** Booking ID generated, Agreement/Possession & Payment statuses updated
- **Result:** Complete revenue trail from booking to collection

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>STEP 6: CLOSURE & REPORTING</b></summary>
