## 7. Design Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use navy as the dominant base and gold as a restrained accent.
- Keep one clear focal point: the headline or the offer price.
- Use real-estate imagery that feels Indian-premium: modern towers, villas, clean interiors, site-visit moments.
- Show product UI as clean floating cards, not busy full screenshots.
- Keep text sizes large enough for mobile viewing.
- Keep 5–8% safe padding on all sides.

### Don't
- Don't use bright multicolor gradients, neon, or startup clichés.
- Don't overcrowd with more than four feature bullets.
- Don't use fake awards, fake client logos, or fake statistics.
- Don't distort the phone number or website for styling.
- Don't translate or paraphrase the supplied copy.
- Don't add "GST", "EMI", "free trial", or any unapproved offer.
